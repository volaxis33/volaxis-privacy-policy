Last updated: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VolAxis (“VolAxis”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, contact us by email, or otherwise express interest in VolAxis.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

VolAxis is the brand responsible for handling the information described in this Policy. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at volaxisx@gmail.com.

Before publishing: identify the legal person or business operating VolAxis and add its full legal name and business address here if applicable. A brand name alone may not be enough to identify the data controller under applicable law.

2. Information we collect

When you complete a VolAxis lead form, we may collect the information fields you choose to submit:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Country

We may also receive basic form-submission information and technical information made available by the platform hosting the form. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through our lead forms.

3. How we use your information

We use the information to:

  • respond to your request and contact you about VolAxis;
  • provide information about VolAxis products, services, access opportunities, announcements or offers where you have requested or agreed to receive them;
  • manage and improve our communications and lead enquiries; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations and protect our rights.

We contact leads by email. We do not use the information collected through these forms to send SMS or WhatsApp messages.

4. Legal basis and your choices

We process information for the purposes explained above, including to respond to a request you make, take steps at your request before a potential purchase or relationship, and where applicable on the basis of your consent or our legitimate interests, subject to your rights under applicable law. Where marketing consent is required, we will ask for it. You can opt out of marketing emails at any time by using an unsubscribe link if provided or by emailing volaxisx@gmail.com.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only as reasonably necessary:

  • with Meta Platforms and its relevant services, because your information is submitted through Facebook or Instagram lead forms;
  • with service providers who help us operate our communications, email, website or business systems, subject to appropriate safeguards and instructions;
  • where required by law, a competent authority, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims; or
  • in connection with a business transfer, where permitted by law and with appropriate protections.

Those providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where applicable, we will take the steps required by relevant data-protection laws for international transfers.

6. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including responding to your enquiry, managing any resulting customer relationship, meeting legal obligations and resolving disputes. When it is no longer needed, we will delete it or securely anonymise it, subject to any legal retention requirement.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information; object to or request restriction of certain processing; withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; or lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority.

To make a request, email volaxisx@gmail.com and explain what you would like us to do. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Children

VolAxis lead forms and communications are not intended for children. Please do not submit personal information if you are not legally permitted to do so.

10. Third-party platforms and links

When you use Facebook or Instagram, Meta also processes information under its own privacy policy and terms. This Policy applies to VolAxis’s handling of information; it does not replace the privacy terms of Meta or other third-party services.
